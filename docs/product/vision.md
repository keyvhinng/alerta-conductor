# Product Vision

**Alerta Conductor** is a B2B preventive safety platform for professional drivers, initially for
interprovincial transport companies in Peru. *(ES: Plataforma preventiva de seguridad para
conductores profesionales.)*

## Problem
Fatigue and distraction raise accident risk. Most monitoring tools react to dangerous behavior
while the driver is already driving.

## Vision
Identify potential risk **before** a shift starts, and keep monitoring risk **during** the trip,
with supervisors in control of the final decisions.

## Differentiation
1. Preventive assessment before driving (wearable sleep/recovery data).
2. Continuous monitoring while driving (on-device computer vision + vehicle telemetry).
3. Centralized risk management for fleet supervisors.

## Positioning / non-goals
Not a navigation app, route optimizer, road-condition monitor, or generic fleet tracker.
Not a medical device: it never determines medical fitness to drive.

## Target users
Drivers, fleet supervisors, company administrators (see [prd.md](prd.md)).

## Pitch (ES)
> Alerta Conductor evita accidentes *antes* de que empiecen: evalúa el descanso del conductor
> antes del turno y vigila señales de fatiga y distracción durante el viaje, avisando al conductor
> y al supervisor a tiempo. Es apoyo a la decisión; la decisión final es siempre del supervisor.
